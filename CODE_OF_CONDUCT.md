# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We as the members, the contributors and the maintainers of this project pledge
to make the participation in the project a free experience for everybody,
without harassment, whatever the age, the body, the disability, the
ethnicity, the gender identity and expression, the level of experience, the
nationality, the personal appearance, the race, the religion or the sexual
identity and orientation of the person.

## Our standards

The behaviour that helps the project:

- being kind and patient, everybody was a beginner once,
- accepting the feedback and thanking the person who gave it,
- focusing on what is good for the users of the project,
- being careful with the secrets of the other people.

The behaviour that is not allowed:

- the harassment, the insults and the personal attacks,
- the sexual words and the attention of a sexual kind,
- the publishing of the private data of somebody without the permission,
- any other behaviour that would make the participation unpleasant.

## The duties of the maintainers

The maintainers are responsible for the clarity of the standards and for
taking a fair correction when the standards are broken. They have the right
and the duty to remove, edit or reject the contributions that do not follow
the standards, and to ban a contributor for the behaviour that is not
acceptable.

## The scope

The code of conduct applies in the spaces of the project: the repository, the
issues, the pull requests and the discussions. It applies also when somebody
represents the project outside of it.

## The enforcement

The problems are reported to the maintainer in private, the same way as the
problems with the security. The maintainer answers as soon as possible and
keeps the report private. The maintainer respects the privacy and the safety
of the person who reports a problem.

## The source

This code of conduct follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1.
